100 days drawing

I'm right-handed. For 100 days, I draw the same object twice: once with my right hand, once with my left.

I've longed for art since I was young. Wherever my eyes rested, art was there. But it always felt like a far-off world. So many people were already good at it. I didn't think I had the talent, or the right. I thought it was too late. My eyes had learned to see well, but my hands were still at the starting line. The distance felt so vast that I kept putting off the start.

So I decided not to go back. I would make a new path from right where I stood. 100 days, one page a day.

A right-handed person drawing with the left hand is bound to draw badly. I wanted to step away from comparing myself to others, so I gave myself a handicap. In a way, I gave myself a reason not to be good.

To keep going every day, I couldn't go far. So I began where my eyes land first: the things in my home. I draw each one with my familiar right hand, then once more with my clumsy left. Two drawings sit side by side on a single page.

Now I don't compare my hands to anyone else's. I just look at my own two, side by side.
